FAQs

Who is the AIC High-Ticket Closer program for?

It's for anyone—whether you're a complete beginner wanting to break into remote sales or a seasoned rep looking to transition into high-ticket closing to scale your income to $10K+/month.

What makes AIC different from other sales training programs?

We don't just give you a course and leave you alone. You get daily live 1-on-1 roleplay, call analysis from 7-figure closers, and direct placement into vetted, high-converting offers.

Do you guarantee a job?

While we cannot legally guarantee employment, we guarantee interviews and placement opportunities within our extensive network of 7- and 8-figure business owners once you pass our internal certification.

What does the support structure look like?

You have access to 24/7 Slack support, daily live Q&A calls, mandatory roleplay sessions, and 1-on-1 access to coaches who actively review your recorded sales calls to provide immediate feedback.

If I'm already in a sales role, how can you help?

We help you transition from low-ticket or traditional B2B/B2C sales into high-ticket inbound closing, where commission checks are significantly larger and call volume is typically warmer.

How long does it typically take to land a sales role?

Most students who dedicate 1-2 hours daily to mastering the frameworks are able to pass certification and land their first high-ticket role within 4 to 8 weeks.

Do I need previous sales experience?

No prior experience is needed. Our blueprint is designed to take complete beginners and mold them into top 1% closers using our proven step-by-step frameworks.

How quickly can I start making $10k/month?

While results vary based on your dedication, most of our students who follow the daily actions and attend the 1:1 sessions hit the $10k/mo run rate within their first 90 to 120 days of placement.

Is this a full-time commitment?

During the training phase, you can start part-time (1-2 hours a day). However, to reach $10k+/mo, you will eventually want to transition to taking sales calls full-time remotely.

How do I get started?

Simply click any "Book A Call" button on this page to schedule a free 1-on-1 consultation with our admissions team to see if you're a good fit for the program.
