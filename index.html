FAQs

1. Is this program for complete beginners?

Absolutely. Most of our students start with little to no sales experience. We begin by helping you develop the mindset and communication skills needed to succeed before moving into objection handling, sales psychology, live roleplays, and everything required to become a high-ticket sales professional.

2. Will you help me find a remote sales job?

Yes. Once you're ready, you'll learn exactly how to position yourself, reach out to quality companies, and stand out during the hiring process. You'll also gain access to our internal network of vetted opportunities, giving you the chance to interview with legitimate offers looking for trained sales talent.

3. How long does it take to start seeing results?

Everyone progresses at a different pace, but the students who consistently attend coaching calls, complete the training, practice roleplays, and take action on outreach typically see results much faster than those who treat the program casually. Your effort directly impacts your outcome.

4. What makes this different from free YouTube content?

YouTube can teach you concepts, but it can't tell you what you're doing wrong. Inside the program, you receive structured training, live coaching, personalized feedback, roleplay sessions, accountability, and a community that pushes you to improve every week. That's what accelerates your progress.

5. Do I need previous sales or business experience?

Not at all. What matters most is your willingness to learn, accept feedback, and put in consistent work. We've seen complete beginners develop into confident setters and closers by following the process.

6. What if I'm willing to work hard but don't know where to start?

That's exactly why this program exists. You'll follow a proven roadmap—from building the right mindset and mastering the sales skillset to learning outreach, interviewing with companies, and landing your first remote opportunity. You won't be left wondering what your next step is.

7. How do I know if this program is right for me?

The first step is a free strategy call. We'll learn about your goals, your current situation, and whether you're a good fit for the mentorship. If we don't believe the program is the right solution for you, we'll tell you honestly. We only work with people who are committed to putting in the work required to succeed.

8. Is landing a job guaranteed?

We can't guarantee that every student will land a role because your results ultimately depend on your commitment and consistency. What we do guarantee is that you'll have access to the training, coaching, accountability, and proven systems that have helped hundreds of students break into remote high-ticket sales. If you show up, practice, and execute the process, you'll put yourself in the strongest possible position to succeed.
